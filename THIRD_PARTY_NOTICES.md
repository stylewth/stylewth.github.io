## The Movie Database (TMDB)

Film data and artwork are provided through the TMDB API. The bundled TMDB mark is an
unmodified approved logo from the official
[TMDB logos and attribution page](https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution).
The attribution requirements are documented in the
[TMDB API FAQ](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq).

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## Spotify

Album metadata and artwork are provided by Spotify oEmbed. The bundled full Spotify
logo is an unmodified official RGB asset. Its attribution, linking, and display
requirements are documented in the
[Spotify Design & Branding Guidelines](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design).
