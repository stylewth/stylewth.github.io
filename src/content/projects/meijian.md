## Problem

Meijian Narrative Intelligence turns market feedback into traceable brand narrative decisions. It connects evidence preparation, candidate evaluation, and iterative revision in one decision workbench.

## Contribution

As the team's full-stack development lead, I designed the system architecture, implemented the Python/Streamlit workbench, and delivered the live demo. The workbench connects data preprocessing, narrative stress testing, and a real-time decision dashboard.

I implemented the AI workflow for corpus analysis, narrative generation and ranking, candidate stress testing, and incremental evidence evolution. I also integrated Feishu bots, interactive cards, and multidimensional tables for milestone notifications, human-in-the-loop decisions, remote step synchronization, and structured result writeback.

## Competition result

National Top 40 in the Feishu 2026 AI Pioneer Future Talent Competition. I led full-stack development from July to September 2026.

The linked repository contains the implementation and a minimal frozen case package.
