## Problem

Meijian Narrative Intelligence turns market feedback into traceable brand narrative decisions. It connects evidence preparation, candidate evaluation, and iterative revision in one decision workbench.

## Contribution

The pipeline prepares and freezes evidence, uses two agents to challenge and revise narrative candidates, and evaluates them with holdout evidence and human blind review. Incremental evidence then updates candidate scores, risks, rankings, and narrative text.

A Streamlit workbench presents the three stages. Feishu integration adds remote step controls, milestone notifications, decision confirmations, and structured result synchronization.

## Current state

The project was submitted to the Feishu AI Innovation Competition and continues to evolve. The public online case replays frozen results; custom model inference and Feishu collaboration run locally with configured credentials.
